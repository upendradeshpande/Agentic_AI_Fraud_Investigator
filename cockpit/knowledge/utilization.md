## Visit plausibility
Check visit verification records (electronic visit verification, timesheets or check-in logs). Frequent visits combined with a long travel distance need an operational explanation, such as a live-in arrangement or a local branch of the provider.

## High-need members
Many prior claims and frequent visits are expected for members with high care needs. Compare against the care plan and the assessed level of need before treating utilization as suspicious. This is the most common false-positive pattern in the synthetic evaluation.

## Distance
Distance is measured between member and provider addresses on file. Confirm the address that services were delivered from; agencies with several branches may register a head-office address.
