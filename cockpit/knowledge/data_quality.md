## Repeated claim numbers
A claim number that appears on more than one case is a data-quality issue to resolve with claims operations. It can be a keying error, a resubmission or a split claim. It is not evidence of fraud and does not add suspicion points.

## Missing or invalid values
Cases with missing or invalid values receive lower confidence. Correct the source record and re-import rather than editing values in the cockpit.
