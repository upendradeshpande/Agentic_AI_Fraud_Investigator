## Amount versus peers
The amount versus peer average field is supplied by the source system; its peer definition is not available here. Compare the billed rate with the contracted rate for the care type before relying on it.

## Weekend and round-dollar billing
Estimated rather than recorded billing often shows as round amounts. Sample a few line items and request the matching timesheets. Weekend care is normal for some care types, so compare with same-care-type peers.

## Several moderate signals
Several moderately unusual signals together are worth a look even when none is alarming on its own. Treat this as a reason to sample records, not as evidence.
