## Interpretation
Binary flags are source-system indicators, not verified events. Continuous signals are compared with the loaded queue using a robust z-score, and peer tables compare with other cases of the same care type. Similar cases describe feature proximity, not a shared outcome.

## Score meaning
The triage score is a review priority from 0 to 100. It is not a fraud probability. Priority, confidence and financial exposure are shown separately.
