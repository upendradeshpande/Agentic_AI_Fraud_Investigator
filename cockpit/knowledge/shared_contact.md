## Verify provenance
Find out which record introduced the shared address or phone and when. Check whether the provider is a registered family caregiver, whether the member lives in a facility the provider operates, or whether a case manager's number was entered for both.

## What it does not prove
Shared contact details can have administrative causes. Do not infer a personal relationship or wrongdoing from the flag alone.

## Recent policy change
A policy change shortly before the claim is weak on its own. It becomes relevant when combined with shared contact details or a new provider relationship.
