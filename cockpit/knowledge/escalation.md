## Before a final decision
Record the source evidence reviewed, unresolved questions, mitigating information and a rationale before escalating or closing. Only an authorised investigator makes a final disposition; the AI may only recommend.

## Choosing a status
Use Needs more information when a specific record is outstanding. Use Likely false positive when a documented explanation accounts for the flagged signals. Escalate when a strong indicator is confirmed against source records.
